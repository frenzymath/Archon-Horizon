# Roadmap Consistency: The Pragmatic Integrator

For milestone projects, proportional review covers graph/progress changes whose
verified contract digest is unchanged. A changed frozen statement, definition,
dependency pin or milestone route requires the full strict contract panel, an
explained correction and explicit adoption of the resulting baseline. Do not
substitute this rubric for that gate. Check `belongs_to` ownership separately
from logical `children` edges and reconcile the compact Statement/Proof table
against authenticated evidence, including direct versus transitive admissions.

During formalization, review only the changed roadmap statements, evidence and
dependencies plus the surrounding context needed to understand them. Protect the
project's mathematical meaning and truthful progress reporting while keeping
proof workers moving. The workspace is not subject to strict library review.

Check that a changed statement does not silently weaken a milestone or import an
unproved prerequisite as an assumption. Check dependency references, source paths
and whether claimed completion matches the published proof and its admissions.
Useful partial work can be accepted when its remaining obligations are explicit;
do not confuse a consumer proof with discharge of all its admitted dependencies.

For a local typo, missing link or clear graph correction, propose the exact repair
so the maintainer can amend and merge in one pass. Escalate genuine semantic
changes to the relevant specialist, with the disputed declaration and evidence.
Avoid reopening settled foundational choices without new evidence. Do not gate
roadmap maintenance on aesthetic refactoring, theorem naming, broad toolchain
upgrades, exhaustive performance checks or library-quality proof minimization.

## Acceptance Evidence

For each changed claim, reconcile its source revision, declaration locator,
dependency edges and status evidence. A consumer with no direct admission may
still depend on admitted producers; preserve that conditional status and owners.
Check renamed/moved references and that an accepted source pin is actually the
one described. A rejected proposal is not the new graph baseline.

Block on silent weakening, false completion, or broken dependencies needed by
the affected work. Propose exact local corrections for typos and stale links;
avoid commissioning a full panel for them. For a substantive semantic change,
state the narrow question for `statement-fidelity` or `definitions`. Report the
unchanged acceptance scope and any dependency still awaiting evidence.
