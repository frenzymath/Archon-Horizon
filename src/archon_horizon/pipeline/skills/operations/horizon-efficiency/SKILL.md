# Keep Work Focused

Choose the next artifact or decision, then fetch the smallest evidence needed
for it. Use the supplied task brief and retained context. Follow truncation and
pagination links when relevant evidence is missing; do not fetch full context,
catalogs or histories as a startup ritual.

- Read assigned declarations, their definitions and immediate consumers before
  expanding to a graph cone or repository audit.
- Reuse a successful check when its exact source, dependencies and checked scope
  still match. Run narrow checks while editing and required targets at publication.
- On a repair round, read the requested changes and diff. Preserve unaffected
  findings and checks; do not restart the full investigation.
- Keep one owner for a coherent result family. Several related PRs can reuse
  its context; a fresh agent for every helper adds handoffs without independence.
- Stop retrying an unchanged failed action. Identify whether it lacks a proof,
  source, publication, toolchain, transport or capacity, then repair that cause.

Ordinary workers own their assigned mathematics and concrete blockers. They do
not repeatedly inspect fleet occupancy, rebalance roles or audit the campaign.
Before delegation, a planning or integration owner checks current owners and
compatible capacity once, then queues only useful independent work. An empty
queue is not a reason to commission an audit. Report a concrete scheduling or
environment problem to the responsible owner through Zulip.

Waiting for an actual external result should release the execution slot. Retain
the owner with an event-conditioned checkpoint when it still has a concrete
integration or repair task; otherwise finish the delivered scope. A retained
workspace has host affinity, so its wake condition must have an independent
producer. See [delegation](../horizon-delegation/SKILL.md).

Keep durable handoffs short: result, evidence and any remaining owner or unlock
event. Link detailed logs instead of copying them into every mission revision.
Use Zulip for a decision, actionable blocker or requested clarification, not a
parallel progress transcript. Efficiency never permits weaker statements,
hidden admissions or unsupported acceptance.
